Smart Rainfall Action Planner

Enter an expected rainfall amount and the tank's current fill level. The planner estimates incoming roof runoff and possible overflow using your calculator settings. This is a scenario tool, not a live weather forecast.

Your action plan

Enter your expected rainfall and current tank level, then press “Plan for this rainfall”.

How the estimate works

Incoming water = roof area × effective rainfall × runoff coefficient × filter efficiency. The first-flush amount is subtracted from the entered rainfall once for this scenario.

Actual collection can differ due to rainfall intensity, roof condition, gutter losses, and the timing of rain. Check that any overflow/recharge outlet is safe and clear.
